# Data Collection Instrument and Methodology

**Study:** Internet Connectivity and Students' Learning Experience on Campus (NS-DTS 223, Topic 2)
**Instrument:** Online questionnaire (Google Forms), "Untitled form", responses exported to Google Sheets

> The question wording below is taken directly from the response sheet. Answer options were **reconstructed from the responses received**. An option that nobody chose (for example *Never* in Q6) may exist on the form but is not visible in the data. Please check against the live form and attach a screenshot or PDF of it to the submission.

## 1. Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Target population | Registered NSUK students who use the internet on the Keffi campus |
| Sampling technique | Non-probability **convenience sampling**; form link shared online with students. *Group to confirm the exact channels used (e.g. class WhatsApp groups)* |
| Target sample size | ≥ 100 (assignment guideline) |
| Achieved sample | **84 valid responses**, all retained after cleaning |
| Collection period | **18–22 November 2026** (26, 19, 11, 23 and 5 responses per day) |
| Mode | Self-administered online questionnaire |
| Variables | Profile (3), access (4), connectivity experience (5), academic impact (4), cost (1), solutions (1), open-ended (2) |
| Ethics | Voluntary and anonymous. No names, matriculation numbers, phone numbers or e-mail addresses collected |
| Known instrument issues | Q8 changed from labelled options to a 1–5 scale on 20 Nov; several single-choice items accepted multiple ticks. Both were resolved in cleaning (see `reports/tables/cleaning_log.csv`) |
| Not collected | Network speed tests and direct observation, which the brief suggests for this topic. Recommended for a follow-up |

## 2. Questionnaire

### Section A: Respondent profile
1. **What is your current level?** 100 Level · 200 Level · 300 Level · 400 Level · 500 Level
2. **What is your faculty?** Natural and Applied Sciences · Social Sciences · Arts · Education · Law · Administration · Other
3. **What is your department?** *(short answer)*

### Section B: Internet access
4. **Which network provider do you primarily use on campus?** *(multiple answers received)* MTN · Glo · Airtel · 9mobile
5. **What type of internet connection do you mostly use?** Mobile data · Campus Wi-Fi · Personal Wi-Fi
6. **How frequently do you use the internet for academic activities?** Always · Very often · Sometimes · (Rarely · Never)
7. **Where do you mostly access the internet on campus?** Library · Faculty buildings · Lecture halls · Hostels · Open spaces · Other

### Section C: Connectivity experience
8. **How would you rate your internet connection on campus?** Very poor · Poor · Average · Good · Excellent *(1–5 scale from 20 Nov)*
9. **How often do you experience poor internet connectivity?** Always · Often · Sometimes · Rarely · (Never)
10. **At what time do you experience the best internet connection?** *(checkbox)* Morning (6 AM–11 AM) · Afternoon (12 PM–3 PM) · Evening (4 PM–7 PM) · Night (8 PM onwards) · No noticeable difference
11. **How often does your internet connection disconnect unexpectedly?** Always · Often · Sometimes · Rarely · Never
12. **How satisfied are you with your network provider's internet speed on campus?** Very satisfied · Satisfied · Neutral · Dissatisfied · Very dissatisfied

### Section D: Academic impact
13. **Which academic activity is most affected by poor internet connectivity?** *(checkbox)* Downloading lecture materials · Online research · Attending online classes · Submitting assignments · Watching educational videos · Online examinations
14. **How does poor internet connectivity affect your academic activities?** Makes online research difficult · Interrupts online classes · Causes delays in completing assignments · Prevents access to learning materials · Makes little or no difference · Other
15. **To what extent does poor internet connectivity affect your academic performance?** Not at all · Slightly · Moderately · Highly · Very highly
16. **Have you ever missed an academic deadline because of poor internet connectivity?** Yes · No

### Section E: Cost and solutions
17. **How much do you spend on internet data weekly?** Below ₦1,000 · ₦1,000–₦2,000 · ₦2,001–₦3,000 · ₦3,001–₦5,000 · Above ₦5,000
18. **What do you think the university should do to improve internet connectivity on campus?** *(checkbox)* Install more Wi-Fi hotspots · Improve existing network infrastructure · Partner with telecommunications providers · Provide affordable internet access · Other

### Section F: Open-ended
19. **Please describe any major internet connectivity challenge you experience on campus.** *(paragraph, optional)*
20. **What additional suggestions do you have for improving students' internet learning experience?** *(paragraph, optional)*

## 3. Recommended instrument improvements (for a repeat study)
* Lock single-choice items as radio buttons, and keep the Q8 format fixed for the whole collection period.
* Make "Department" a dropdown to avoid spelling variants.
* Add a structured observation sheet for **speed tests** (location, date, time, provider, download/upload Mbps, latency) at fixed campus points.
* Use quota sampling across faculties and levels to reduce the dominance of computing departments.
